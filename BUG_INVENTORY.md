## Fix policy

- Critical and High issues block a production release.
- Security fixes may intentionally reject behavior that was previously allowed;
  the change and denial tests must be recorded.
- A fix must address the root cause, include regression consideration, and be
  verified at the correct boundary. A build alone does not verify auth, SQL,
  RLS, storage, or deployed routing.
- Unknown production behavior is a blocker, not permission to infer or delete.
